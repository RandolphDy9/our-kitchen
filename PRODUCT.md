# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Club pickleball players and the volunteers who run their tournaments. Both groups are mostly on phones, standing at the venue between games: players scan a QR code to check in, watch for "You're up!", look up their court, and check pools and the bracket. Organizers import the Excel schedule, enter scores and manage admins, usually from a phone and occasionally from a laptop during setup. Many players are older, so text has to be comfortable to read at arm's length.

## Product Purpose
OurKitchen runs a one-day pickleball tournament from a single link: check-in, live courts, pools, playoff brackets, alerts, reactions and an event photo gallery. Success means no one has to crowd around a printed sheet or shout across the gym. Players know where to be and when, and organizers spend their time on the courts instead of the paperwork.

## Brand Personality
Bold, sporty, friendly. It feels like a club event poster: confident yellow, black and white, square edges, punchy all-caps headings and hard shadows. The copy is plain and warm, like a helpful club volunteer, never corporate.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards: soft gradients, rounded pastel cards, tiny gray text.
- Sportsbook / betting apps: neon on black, dense numbers, flashing urgency.
- Cluttered tournament software with tables everywhere and desktop-only layouts.

## Design Principles
1. **Phone at the court first.** Every screen works one-handed on a small phone; larger screens get more room, not a different app.
2. **Next action is obvious.** Each screen leads with what the person needs right now (my court, my next game, check in).
3. **Readable at a glance.** Generous type sizes and strong contrast, so a player can read it in a quick look between points.
4. **Loud identity, calm structure.** The bold skin carries the personality; layout, spacing and hierarchy stay disciplined and predictable.
5. **Plain words.** Labels and messages say exactly what happens, in the club's own language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at least 16px on phones, no labels smaller than about 12px, touch targets at least 44px. Respect reduced-motion preferences (already wired in). No color-only status signals.
